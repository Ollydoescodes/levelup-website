How it works

One room, four ways to use it

Walk in, grab a seat, and pay for your time at the counter. Everything below is included. Order food and drinks upstairs and the kitchen sends them up.

Play

Hundreds of games, off the shelf

Board, card and party games for every group and age. New to one? Ask at the counter and someone will teach you. Magic: The Gathering nights and a card store are on the way.

Work

Quiet, cool coworking

Air-con, fast wifi and free-flow tea, coffee and water make it an easy place to get a day's work done. Need to focus or take a call? Book the private room by the hour.

Gather

A social home, not a screen

Games nights, one-shot D&D, kids' sessions and a community that keeps coming back. Bring a team, a family or just yourself. There's always a table and usually a game already going.

Refuel

Free-flow drinks & the full kitchen

Your time includes self-serve tea, coffee, water and the soda fountain. Hungry? Order the whole Nomad menu, plus our own pizzas, from the counter and it comes up from the kitchen below.

Private room

Enquire about the private room

The quiet room off the main floor, for focused work, a call, a meeting or a game you want to yourselves. $8 an hour for the room, on top of normal entry; Premium members get 4 hours free every month. Send an enquiry and the desk confirms your slot by message, so there's never a double-booking.

Ask the desk

Tell us when you'd like the room and we'll check it's free. Same-day? Give the desk a call to be safe.

What happens next
1

You send your enquiry

Your request opens in Telegram, ready to send to the Level Up desk, with date, time and party size all filled in.

2

The desk checks the room

Someone looks at the day and makes sure the private room is actually free. No clashes, no surprises.

3

We confirm by message

You get a reply confirming the slot (and, for longer bookings, whether a small deposit is needed). That's your booking locked in.

Why not instant? One quiet room means we confirm every booking by hand. It's the surest way to avoid two groups turning up for the same slot. Prefer to talk? Message us on Telegram or call the desk any time we're open.
