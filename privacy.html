Privacy Policy

Level Up Social Hub and Nomad Eatery, Siem Reap, Cambodia. Last updated 19 August 2026.

This policy covers levelupsiemreap.com, which is home to two sister venues in Siem Reap: Level Up Social Hub upstairs, and Nomad Eatery downstairs. Both are run by the same team, and both pages of this site are covered here.

The short version. This is a plain information website. We do not run analytics, we set no cookies of our own, we have no advertising trackers, and there is nothing to sign up for. Nothing you type into the enquiry form reaches us unless you choose to send it.

The enquiry form

The private room enquiry form on the Level Up page does not submit to a server. There is no database behind it. When you fill it in and press one of the buttons, your browser builds a short message from what you entered and hands it to whichever app you picked:

  • Enquire on Telegram opens Telegram with the message ready. Your browser also copies the message to your clipboard so you can paste it if needed. We receive it only once you press send in Telegram.
  • Enquire by email opens your own email app with the message ready. We receive it only once you press send.

If you do send us an enquiry, we use it to check the room is free and confirm your booking. We keep it only as long as we need to handle that booking and any follow-up, and we do not use it for marketing or share it with anyone else.

Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare records standard technical information when a page is served, which can include your IP address, browser type and the page requested. This is used to serve the site, keep it available and protect it from abuse. We do not use these logs to build any profile of you.

Fonts

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. That means your browser makes a request to Google's servers, and Google receives your IP address as part of it. We do not send Google anything else about you, and no font-related cookie is set on this site.

Cookies

We set no cookies. There is no consent banner on this site because there is nothing to consent to.

Links to other services

The site links out to Instagram, Facebook, Google Maps and Telegram. Once you follow one of those links you are on their service, and their own privacy policies apply, not ours. The Level Up membership card runs inside Telegram and is a separate thing from this website.

Photographs

The photographs on this site were taken in our venues and are published with the agreement of the people in them. If you appear in a photo here and would like it removed, email us and we will take it down.

Your information

If you have sent us an enquiry and would like to know what we hold, or want it deleted, just ask and we will do it. Because we collect so little, this is usually a matter of finding one message and removing it.

Contact

Or come and find us. Level Up is upstairs, Nomad is downstairs, Wat Damnak, Krong Siem Reap.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add something that collects information, such as an online booking system or analytics, we will update this page before it goes live and change the date at the top.
